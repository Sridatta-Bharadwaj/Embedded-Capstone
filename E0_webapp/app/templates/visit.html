How the confidence is computed

The model outputs a raw probability of {{ '%.4f'|format(v.prob) }}. Its decision threshold is {{ '%.4f'|format(v.threshold) }}, chosen on validation data to favour catching cancers. Raw scores are not calibrated, so the app shifts them so the threshold sits at 50%. That gives a malignancy score of {{ v.score|pct(1) }}. Confidence is that score (malignant) or 100% minus it (benign). Treat it as a measure of distance from the boundary, not a clinical probability.
