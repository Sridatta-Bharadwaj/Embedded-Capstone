Reading a result

  • Tumour type. The model can say only “high-grade serous carcinoma” or “not”. It does not name benign subtypes.
  • Confidence. Raw scores are rescaled so the threshold sits at 50%. Confidence is the distance from that boundary, not a calibrated clinical probability.
  • Outline. A Grad-CAM map from the network's last feature layer (7 × 7, upsampled). It shows where the evidence came from, not the tumour's exact edge.
  • Twin risk. An exponentially smoothed average of a patient's scan scores, with an alert on sharp rises or whenever the latest scan scores malignant.

Limitations

  • Only 50 malignant training images, all of one cancer type, from one hospital. Behaviour on other scanners is unknown.
  • Roughly 1 in 3 cancers in testing was missed, and most alarms were false (PPV ≈ 18%).
  • Grad-CAM showed the model sometimes attends to on-screen artefacts, such as inpainted measurement boxes. Check the outline.
  • A research prototype for a student project. It is not a medical device and not for diagnosis.
