How the twin works

Each scan gives a malignancy score, where 50% is the model's decision boundary. The twin keeps a running risk: risk = 0.5 × new score + 0.5 × previous risk. One odd scan moves it only halfway, while a run of suspicious scans pushes it up. The twin raises an alert when risk jumps by more than 15 points between scans, crosses the boundary upwards, or the latest scan itself scores malignant. Scans are ordered by scan date.
